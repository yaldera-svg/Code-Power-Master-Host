Tech Hero

This page highlights entrepreneur, consultant, and assistant professor, Janeya Griffin, and the tech-industry pioneer, Kimberly Bryant, and their contributions to the industry.

Who is Janeya Griffin?

Janeya Griffin, also known as "The Commercializer," is a Strategic Consultant who focuses on helping new entrepreneurs in minority communities utilize their skills and intellectual assets. Born to two incarcerated parents, Griffin had humble beginnings that motivated her to advocate for those who were dealt a bad hand. She is the founder of Equity Space Alliance, which emphasizes the importance of equitable, diverse, and inclusive practices in the space-tech industry.

Notable Awards:

  • NASA's Inclusive Space Tech Catalyst Prize
  • NASA Early Career Public Achievement Medal
  • 2016 FLC Far West Laboratory Representative of the Year

Who is Kimberly Bryant?

Kimberly Bryant an engineer and the founder of Black Girls CODE, a nonprofit organization that focuses on teaching young black girls and girls of color ages 7-17 skills in technology and computer programming. Her efforts helped give young, underrepresented individuals' knowledge of building and developing technology. Her organization empowers young girls to learn entrepreneurial and coding skills that were historically gate kept. Massively successful, Bryant held leadership roles at some of the biggest companies in the world like Genentech, Merck, and Pfizer. Her work is deeply intersectional because she took on a field that historicall excluded black people AND women and disseminated it to the next generation.

Notable Awards:

  • Jefferson Award for Community Service
  • BusinessInsider's 2013 Top 25 Most Influential African-Americans in Technology
  • Pioneer in Tech Award
