Tech Hero

This page highlights entrepreneur, consultant, and assistant professor, Janeya Griffin, and her contributions to the tech industry.

Who is Janeya Griffin?

Janeya Griffin, also known as "The Commercializer" is a Strategic Consultant who focuses on helping new entrepreneurs in minority communities utilize their skills and intellectual assets. She is the founder of, Equity Space Alliance, which emphasizes the importance of equitable, diverse, and inclusive practices in the space-tech industry.

Notable Awards:

  • NASA's Inclusive Space Tech Catalyst Prize
  • NASA Early Career Public Achievement Medal
  • 2016 FLC Far West Laboratory Representative of the Year
