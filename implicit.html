Implicit Bias: Educational Resources

This page explores the concept of implicit bias and its impact on our daily lives.

A Brief Overview of Implicit Bias and Intersectionality:

Implicit bias refers to the attitudes or stereotypes that trigger our unconscious attitudes and decisions related to a specific social group.

Intersectionality examines how various social identities such as race, gender, and class interact to create an individual's unique experiences.

Implicit Bias Tests

The Gender-Career Implicit Association Test

Created by researchers at Harvard University, the Gender-Career IAT is an IBT designed to measure implicit biases related to gender and career.

The Race Implicit Association Test

Also developed by researchers at Harvard University, the Race IAT measures implicit biases related to race and ethnicity.
