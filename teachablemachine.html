Our Project Statement

Project statment: *im revising this but here are the main points* Lesson 1: quality training data. Despite having many images, the machine still needed careful attention to detail to the quality of images it trained on. This model required images that were taken directly on the website using the sample feature that the machine recognized. Though tedious, the photos taken, even if similar, were not learnable. On a larger scale, this mirrors the core struggle in Unmasking AI that Joy Buolamwini found in the facial recognition software. The software was trained on poor visual data. It had plenty of quality caucasian photos to train from, but the programmers overlooked diverse communities. The machine is only as good as the humans that teach it. Lesson 2: Learning is doing. It's difficult to learn an issue without experimentation. Joy Buolamwini faced a massive obstacle when she encountered a software that was unable to read her face unless she wore a featureless white mask. As an experienced programmer herself, Joy knew that there was something more malignant under the surface. Her work exposed a larger issue of what centuries of underrepresentation has done to the current tech industry. Stuff about us... Lesson 3: The world of technology is not doomed. With technology radpidly evolving and more accessible than ever before, the deterministic rhetoric that some people assume is challenged by Buolamwini's work and the work of many others. On a small scale, this project taught a simple but valuable lesson that everything can be revised. Despite struggling with new technology and having to work through issues on a topic neither of us were familiar with, we never had to do anything above minor convenience to get the machine in working order.
